# Pause and restart pilot protocol

Status: prepared, not activated. No recruitment, participant data collection, reminders, charges or observed outcomes have occurred. This protocol supersedes the foundation research document's deferred cloud/purchase scope; its qualitative evidence and alternatives remain valid. Engineering test accounts and synthetic payments are excluded from every research result.

## Decision and enrollment

The question is whether a maker leaves enough information at a natural pause to restart confidently at least a week later, with little explanation or prompting. Recruit 15 consenting adults with at least four unfinished crochet, knitting or similar craft projects. Each must expect a natural pause and restart during the 21-day observation window. Record current method (tag, notebook, notes/photo app or craft app), craft, unfinished-project count, platform, recent restart difficulty and recruitment source under a study code. Include makers who prefer their current method; do not require enthusiasm for Loopmark.

Keep the denominator at 15. Record withdrawals, technical failures and missing observations. If fewer than eight people have a restart opportunity, retain the original counts and label restart evidence inconclusive. Do not extend the observation window after seeing results. A later cohort needs a new dated protocol. This small purposive study does not establish population prevalence or a statistically reliable price comparison.

Before enrollment, the release owner must qualify each offered platform, provide working support/privacy/deletion contacts, approve operating funding and specify the exact one-time offer and refund terms. Obtain explicit authorization for recruitment and actual payments. No account credentials or participant contact details belong in this repository.

## Observation schedule

| Time | Activity | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 0 | Explain consent and data handling; show one pause/resume example; observe one checkpoint and a test backup restoration | Capture seconds, facts saved, instructions/rescues and whether the maker understands a QR is a shortcut, not a backup |
| Days 1–14 | Voluntary use on the maker's real projects; technical support only when requested | Natural pause, useful checkpoint, abandonment, prompt source and help minutes; no behavioral reminders |
| Days 8–21 | Observe or obtain a concrete account of a restart at least 168 hours after the checkpoint | Saved timestamp, restart timestamp, first physical action, missing facts, other references used, confidence before/after and recovery seconds |
| After demonstrated value | Present the cohort's preset deliverable offer without pressure | Offer shown, actual settled purchase, refund/dispute and reason to decline; no reward contingent on purchase |
| Day 21 | Close and analyze all 15 study codes | Counts, denominator, missingness, prompting/support burden, current-method comparison and decision |

A scheduled check-in can collect facts but cannot instruct the participant to save or resume. Log any exception and exclude the prompted event from voluntary counts. The onboarding checkpoint never counts as voluntary. A restart button press alone does not prove a physical restart. Time measurements start when the maker begins capture or opens the project to resume; record interruptions separately.

## Prespecified decisions

Continue exploration when **10/15 voluntarily capture**, **8/15 restart after at least seven days**, and **5/15 actually purchase**. All three thresholds must be visible together. Count a voluntary capture only when it contains a usable next action; identify missing tool/position details where relevant. Count purchases only after provider capture/settlement evidence from the actual offer; report later refunds separately and recalculate retained purchasers. Sandbox transactions, founder reimbursements, promises and upgrade clicks never count.

Below 10 captures or eight restarts: hold broad promotion and investigate capture/onboarding/context failures against the participant's current method. If purchases are weak: document the capacity, price and value decision before paid promotion. Do not obscure weak voluntary use with reminders. Even a passing cohort requires an operating and platform release decision; it is not proof of product-market fit.

Report median/range capture and recovery time, voluntary repeats, abandonment reasons, confidence changes, cross-device use, missing media, backup comprehension and support minutes. With 15 people, do not substitute percentile charts or causal claims for the observed records. Analyze lighter-use prospects in a separate exploratory group and keep them outside the enriched cohort's denominator.

## Offer and prices

Three active projects free; finishing frees a slot. The one-time upgrade permits additional active projects within documented technical limits. Existing saved notes/history/export remain accessible. Do not promise unlimited cloud storage or perpetual funded hosting. Publish the service-continuity terms before sale.

The user's proposed experiments are India ₹299–₹599 and a separate international US$4.99–US$7.99 test. The study owner selects one price, currency, tax presentation and channel per cohort before enrollment. Display the actual provider/store price. Do not split 15 participants across several prices and declare a winner. The synthetic INR49900 catalog used by engineering is not a price decision or live product.

## Data minimization and retention

Use an access-controlled study sheet outside git with anonymous codes. Keep consent/contact lookup separate. Collect summarized facts, timestamps and outcomes; avoid craft photos, voice recordings, complete backups and raw payment identifiers. If a specific artifact is necessary, obtain separate informed permission, explain its contents and set a deletion date before receipt. Allow withdrawal without affecting access to purchased features or the participant's own exports.

The study owner records who can access research data, its deletion date and how withdrawal is handled before recruitment. Do not silently add product telemetry. Aggregate results committed here must omit identifying narratives and payment/receipt capabilities. The app's cloud deletion and the research sheet's deletion are separate responsibilities.

## Demonstration and recruitment draft

Prepare a 45–60 second demonstration: pause a real project; show the actual capture duration; return after a genuine week-long interval; follow the saved next step. Label the time jump and any sample data. Include a brief backup explanation. No edited demonstration may imply measured speed or retention that did not occur.

Draft invitation: “Do you have four or more unfinished craft projects? We're testing whether a short checkpoint makes a week-later restart easier. The study lasts 21 days. Participation is optional, and you can keep using your current method. We'll explain data collection, the optional paid offer and how to withdraw before you decide.” Add the approved contact, compensation (if any), dates and terms only when supplied. This text is not permission to contact people. Check each community's current promotion/research rules before authorized outreach.
